# integration — independent audit worksheet

## Q1 (Basic Integration — easy)

$$\int (x^2 + 3x) \, dx$$

## Q2 (Basic Integration — medium)

$$\int \sqrt{x} \, dx$$

## Q3 (Basic Integration — hard)

$$\int \frac{x^4 - 1}{x^2 + 1} \, dx$$

## Q4 (U-Substitution — easy)

$$\int x e^{x^2} \, dx$$

## Q5 (U-Substitution — medium)

$$\int \frac{\ln x}{x} \, dx$$

## Q6 (U-Substitution — hard)

$$\int \frac{x}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} \, dx$$

## Q7 (Integration by Parts — easy)

$$\int x \sin x \, dx$$

## Q8 (Integration by Parts — medium)

$$\int x \ln x \, dx$$

## Q9 (Integration by Parts — hard)

$$\int x^2 \sin(2x) \, dx$$

## Q10 (Trigonometric Integrals — easy)

$$\int \cos^2 x \, dx$$

## Q11 (Trigonometric Integrals — medium)

$$\int \sin^2 x \cos^3 x \, dx$$

## Q12 (Trigonometric Integrals — hard)

$$\int \sin(3x) \cos(2x) \, dx$$

## Q13 (Trig Substitution — easy)

$$\int \frac{x}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} \, dx$$

## Q14 (Trig Substitution — medium)

$$\int \frac{1}{x^2\sqrt{x^2 + 4}} \, dx$$

## Q15 (Trig Substitution — hard)

$$\int \frac{1}{(x^2 + 1)^{3/2}} \, dx$$

## Q16 (Partial Fractions — easy)

$$\int \frac{1}{x^2 - 1} \, dx$$

## Q17 (Partial Fractions — medium)

$$\int \frac{x^2 + 1}{x(x-1)^2} \, dx$$

## Q18 (Partial Fractions — hard)

$$\int \frac{x^3 + 2x^2 + 3x + 1}{x^2(x^2 + 1)} \, dx$$

## Q19 (Improper Integrals — easy)

$$\int_0^{1} \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}} \, dx$$

## Q20 (Improper Integrals — medium)

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{1}{1+x^2} \, dx$$

## Q21 (Improper Integrals — hard)

$$\int_1^{\infty} \frac{\ln x}{x^2} \, dx$$
